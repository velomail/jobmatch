You are designing JobMatch — a Canada-first weekly job matcher. This brief is open-ended. Invent the layout, type, motion, and composition. Do not clone Indeed, LinkedIn, or a generic job board. Do not lock into one card pattern. Propose a UI that feels like a weekly shortlist, not a search engine.

## Product in one line

Stop searching. Start matching.

A person uploads a resume once and gets ten jobs worth their time. Free is that first list. Founding Pro is $9/month for a new Top 10 every week from the same resume. The list is weekly. The bill is monthly. Never write $9/day.

## What this is

An editorial, mobile-first PWA. Open it, today’s roles are already there. Review. Apply on the employer’s careers page. Close the app.

## What this is not

- A job board, feed, or live search box
- Sponsored placements or Easy Apply chrome
- Indeed blue (#2557a7), logo squares, document-icon tiles, “via Indeed” chips
- Blurred “3 of 10” lock walls
- Generated cover letters, talking points, or “send this today”
- A second product called deeper search or career-page inventory
- Desktop marketing chrome inside the app (no SEO footer, no “Canada job search” links)

## Two surfaces (keep them distinct)

1. Marketing site — simple, confident, download-first. Hero, one preview of today’s list, one line on $9/month, App Store / Play, quieter “Open the web app.” Waitlist email is optional and must not dump people into onboarding.
2. The app — phone-first, full viewport, safe areas. On desktop, still feel like an app (you choose how: full-bleed, centered column, device frame, or something better). Bottom tabs: Matches, Results, Account.

## Flow to design (invent the screens)

Onboarding (2–3 beats) → email (magic-link shaped) → resume once (camera / files / paste, large targets) → notifications (“ping when this week’s 10 are ready”) → this week’s list → one role → apply on the company site.

Later days (Pro): open the app, the list is already there. No re-upload.

Stale free week: last week’s list stays readable. One pay moment: “Unlock this week’s 10 — $9/month.” Paying rematches immediately. No second onboarding.

## Listing content (facts only — you decide the form)

Each role can use:

- When it was posted, in plain English (today / yesterday / 3 days ago)
- Company is hiring [title] for remote Canada / hybrid in [city] / on-site in [city]
- Fit to the resume (percent or another honest signal you invent)
- Skills on the resume vs what the listing still asks
- Applicant count when we have it
- A quiet source label for credibility (LinkedIn, Indeed, or Company site) — discovery only, never a destination
- Apply goes only to the employer careers page. Never LinkedIn, Indeed, or another board.

Newest listings can lead. Chronological, plain English is welcome. Keywords people actually want: jobs for my resume, worth your time, next role, apply today, remote jobs Canada, hybrid Toronto, company careers.

Do not invent job titles as ads. Sample Canada companies if you need data: Shopify, Wealthsimple, Ada, Hootsuite, Clio, RBC.

## Desire, not decoration

The user wants a next role without living in a feed. Paid should feel like unlocking today, not buying a feature pack. Copy stays short, honest, Canada-first.

## Brand you can bend

Keep the spirit: purple (oklch ~0.45 0.18 264), near-white paper, Geist / a sharp grotesque + mono for numbers and dates, glass or quiet material, generous type. You may change hierarchy, density, motion, and how the shortlist is shown. Explore magazine, journal, telegram, ritual dashboard, or something we have not tried — as long as it does not become a board.
